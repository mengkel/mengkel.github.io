Welcome! I am an [N3AS](https://n3as.berkeley.edu/) Postdoc Fellow working on **theoretical nuclear astrophysics** at UC Berkeley. My research sits at the intersection of astrophysics, nuclear physics, and machine learning, exploring how exotic nuclear properties shape cosmic phenomena like *r*-process nucleosynthesis and kilonova.

I received my PhD from Clemson University in December 2023, under the supervision of Prof. Bradley Meyer. As a Postdoc Fellow, I am co-mentored by Rebecca Surman (University of Notre Dame), Gail McLaughlin (NC State University) and Dan Kasen (UC Berkeley).
